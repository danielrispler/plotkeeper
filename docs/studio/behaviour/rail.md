# The story rail

The rail, on the window's left, tells the selected session's story, ranked by what Daniel needs: what the agent is doing now, the facts the hooks and git recorded under it, and the earlier turns folded below. Its terms are in `docs/studio/CONTEXT.md`.

**The header** is two lines that wrap and are never cut. The first is the state, with the kind of block when it needs you (question, plan or permission), and the permission mode unless it is the default; the second is the session's name; while it runs, a third gives the estimate. The agent's logo leads the first line; hovering it shows the model line, such as "Claude · Opus 5.5" (not for a subagent), and, for an agent that reports no context share, such as agy, a note saying so. The times show on hover, and the "⋯" menu holds Open (an ended session), Rename, Mark unseen, Close and Delete, and Open all and Close all.

**Now, by state.** The newest turn is the top block of the feed, and what it shows depends on the session's state; a card belongs to its turn and is never drawn above it.

- Running: "You asked" with your prompt, then the intention, boxed, with its age and the step line. The intention is the latest thing the agent wrote in its own words; it shows only while the session runs, and small, as "Why it asks", while it needs you. At your turn the Headline says what happened, and the turn-end intention is the final response's first line, never the summary's.
- Needs you: the docked question, plan or permission, then "Why it asks" and "So far this turn", the facts the hooks recorded.
- Your turn, idle or ended: "You asked", the **Headline**, the evidence line, the Body lines, the Quiz, one line for each subagent and its checks, and "Full response", folded. A session returned to after a while opens on "Where we were" (see [The Brief and Where we were](brief.md)).

**The turn summary.** When a turn ends, one `claude -p` call (the model and effort from `summaries` in `settings.json`; with `on_open` true, when you open the turn instead) writes a Headline and up to three Body lines, for a turn whose final response is about 150 words or more or that ends asking you something. Any other turn shows its response as it is: its Headline is the response's first line, which is also what the Headline is until the summary arrives.

The writer is given the turn's recorded facts (its files with their line counts, hunks up to a cap, each check's command, result and retries) and told not to restate them. The Headline is at most 16 words, an outcome or what the turn waits on; for a turn that ends by asking you, the ask. A Body line is `why`, `reported`, `open` or `ask`, at most 20 words. `open` and `ask` lines always show; `why` and `reported` lines wait for the Quiz to be answered or skipped, and show at once when the turn has no Quiz; a `reported` line reads "Agent says:".

Code, not the prompt, holds the writer to the facts (`understanding/src/grounding.rs`): a Headline that claims a fix, a pass or a verification with no check of the turn that ended passed, or with one that ended failed, starts with "Agent says:"; a Headline or line with a count, percentage, issue or PR id or commit SHA that the turn's material lacks is stripped, a Headline asked for once more, and a stripped second one is replaced by the response's first line. Numbers in the diff, the line counts or a check's result, number words and digits in a known path always pass.

A summary stored before the Headline is read with its first line as the Headline and the rest as `reported` lines; the writer is not run on it again.

**The evidence line**, under the Headline, states what the hooks and git recorded, with no model in it: `3 files · 2 checks passed`. A check counts by its last run, so a failure the agent fixed does not show; only a check that ended failed is named (`✗ cargo test failed`), and one that only "ran" is counted apart. One click opens it in place: each file with its line counts (+added −removed), "+N external" for the changes in the worktree that the agent did not make, and each check with its status ("passed after 1 retry").

A failed check's row opens its exit code and the last 40 lines of its output. A running turn shows only a check that ended failed. A turn's files are the diff between the git trees the hook recorded at its start and end, kept to the files its agent or its workers edited; the ended turn's trees are kept under `refs/plotkeeper/turns/`, so `git gc` leaves its hunks. An agent that reports no trees (agy) has no evidence line and no hunks.

**Real code.** A file row opens its real hunk between the turn's trees, in place, at most about 40 lines; a capped hunk's "N more lines" opens the turn's whole diff, and "Open in editor" opens the file in your editor at the hunk's first line. The palette's "studio::OpenFullDiff" opens the whole diff of the newest turn that has files, in the terminal column's place, one fold per file with its hunk read whole, the external changes in one folded line and "Terminal" to go back.

**Earlier turns** fold to one line each: your prompt, the Headline, a dot for a Quiz not answered yet and a mark for a check that ended failed. A click opens the turn's block under it and folds it again. Opening or closing the evidence line, a hunk, a Quiz's answer or a folded turn changes only that node: the rail's others are kept, the scroll position does not move, and what is open stays open when the studio's state refreshes.

**Ask** sits on a file row, a failed check's row, a subagent's line and the Headline, and the palette's "studio::AskAbout" opens the newest turn's Headline box. Opening it shows what the step ledger and git recorded about the thing (for a file: your prompt, the steps that changed it and its hunk; for a check, its command, exit code and the end of its output; for a subagent, its prompt and steps; for the Headline, your prompt, its files and its checks), at once and with no model.

A suggested question or one you type goes to the card writer's `claude -p` with those facts, and its answer shows under "Interpretation", apart from the recorded facts; the session never sees it. On a file or the Headline, "Why didn't it touch this?" takes a file or a name and shows what was recorded first (read, searched or mentioned in the turn), then the writer's possible reasons folded under "possible reasons (not recorded)"; with nothing recorded there are no reasons, and no call. There is no per-turn "ask about this".

**The narrow window.** The top bar shows each item whole or moves it into the "⋯" menu, never cut mid-word. The session's header (its state dot, state and name) comes first, after the traffic lights, and a click on it toggles the rail; the needs-you chip stays, and so does the settings gear, the bar's last item, right of "+" and "⋯". As the window narrows the menu takes, in order, the usage button, the new-build button, "+" and the git line.

When the window is too narrow for the rail beside the terminal (the width rule, unchanged), the rail is a layer over the terminal: resting the pointer on the session's header or on the spine at the window's left edge for 300 ms opens it, leaving it closes it after 400 ms, and a mouse button held down never opens it. A click pins it open; Esc and a click outside close it. A peek marks its turn seen only after 2 seconds open. The list's strip uses the same delays. The window's minimum width is 480 px.
