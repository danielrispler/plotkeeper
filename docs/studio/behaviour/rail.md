# The story rail

The rail, on the window's left, tells the selected session's story, ranked by what Daniel needs: what the agent is doing now, the facts the hooks and git recorded under it, and the earlier turns folded below. Its terms are in `docs/studio/CONTEXT.md`.

**Over the map** (#385), the rail is not a column: pressing a session's row, in the session list or in a session badge's list on the map, opens the same story in the left panel floating over the map's left side, in the part sheet's glass, with × (named "Close") at its top right, on the header's line (#571) ([The Map view](map.md), "The left panel"). The rail's own element moves into the panel and back, so everything below holds there too. Its turns count as seen while the panel shows it. A second press on the row, or Esc once the drawer, a scrubbed commit and the sheet are closed, closes it and selects no session; leaving the Map view puts the rail back in its column. With no session picked, the rail says "Pick a session in the list." and, under it, "Its story shows here: what it is doing, and each finished turn." (#526), whatever the Explanations switch says; since the panel closes with the selection, that screen is not drawn over the map today.

**The header** is two lines (#444). The first is the agent's logo, the session's name and the "⋯" menu; the name stays on one line and, when it is too long for the rail, ends in "…", by CSS only, with the full name in the page, scrolling into view in place on hover (cut text, [The session list](sessions.md)). The second, muted and on one line, is the state, with the kind of block when it needs you (question, plan or permission), the permission mode unless it is the default, and, while it runs, the estimate, joined by "·": only the items it has, one "·" between each two, so a session with no block kind or no estimate never reads "Running ·" (spec #658). The estimate reads "Implementing · est. 16m–29m left", "est. up to 12m left" once its lower bound has passed, and "Implementing · past its estimate" once its upper bound has. The state keeps the needs-you colour, and the state and the mode never shrink; at a narrow rail the block's kind and the estimate end in "…", each scrolling its full text into view on hover. Hovering the logo shows the model line, such as "Claude · Opus 5.5" (not for a subagent), and, for an agent that reports no context size, such as agy, a note saying so. The headline's hint holds the phase and estimate in full, when there is one, then the times and the model, counted when the header is drawn, and the "⋯" menu holds Rename, Mark unseen (at your turn, once seen) and Delete, which asks first (#447). The menu opens under its button, its end edge at the button's, and flips over the button, or to its other side, when it would leave the window; it is a group of buttons in a popover, placed by CSS anchor positioning from its button (Chrome 157+). It has no Open all or Close all: Chrome's find already searches a turn's folded sections and opens one on a match, and an earlier turn's content is built only when it opens, so unfolding everything never helped find. The left panel's × stays where it is.

**Now, by state.** The newest turn is the top block of the feed, and what it shows depends on the session's state. No card is drawn: cards are retired (danielrispler/plotkeeper#348), and a PR's brief is the explanation ([PR briefs](pr-briefs.md)).

- Running: "You asked" with your prompt, then the intention, boxed, with its age and the step line. The intention is the latest thing the agent wrote in its own words; it shows only while the session runs, and small, as "Why it asks", while it needs you. At your turn the Headline says what happened, and the turn-end intention is the final response's first line, never the summary's.
  While it runs, the box's last line, under the step and set apart by a hair line, is a **Wait tip** (#523, spec #520): "Tip: …", muted and small, one line ending in "…" when cut, its full text scrolling into view on hover. It teaches one feature, naming its palette action with the key your keymap binds it to now ("in the palette" when unbound). One tip shows per Running stretch and stays for it; it is gone at Needs you, Your turn and Idle. Among the tips whose context holds (the shown project has a map, a PR brief waits in the tray, the agent records each turn's files) that have shown fewer than 5 times and are out of cooldown, the one shown longest ago, or never, wins. The cooldown is `max(1, min(4, eligible − 1))` Running stretches, counted at the start of every stretch while tips are on, so a tip never shows twice in a row and a lone eligible tip shows every other stretch. The list is in `ui/tips.ts`; the show history (the stretch counter and each tip's count and last stretch) is in the page's `localStorage`, so clearing it starts tips again. `"tips": false` in `settings.json` turns them off ([Settings files](../settings.md)).
- Needs you: the docked question, plan or permission, then "Why it asks" and "So far this turn", the facts the hooks recorded.
- Your turn, idle or ended: "You asked", the **Headline**, the evidence line, the Body lines, one line for each subagent and its checks, and "Full response", folded. A session returned to after a while opens on "Where we were" (see [The Brief and Where we were](brief.md)).

**The turn summary.** When a turn ends, one `claude -p` call (the model and effort from `summaries` in `settings.json`; with `on_open` true, when you open the turn instead) writes a Headline and up to three Body lines, for a turn whose final response is about 150 words or more or that ends asking you something. Any other turn shows its response as it is: its Headline is the response's first line, which is also what the Headline is until the summary arrives.

The writer is given the turn's recorded facts (its files with their line counts, hunks up to a cap, each check's command, result and retries) and told not to restate them. The Headline is at most 16 words, an outcome or what the turn waits on; for a turn that ends by asking you, the ask. A Body line is `why`, `reported`, `open` or `ask`, at most 20 words. The whole Body shows at once, since a turn's own Quiz is retired (danielrispler/plotkeeper#347); a `reported` line reads "Agent says:".

Code, not the prompt, holds the writer to the facts (`understanding/src/grounding.rs`): a Headline that claims a fix, a pass or a verification with no check of the turn that ended passed, or with one that ended failed, starts with "Agent says:"; a Headline or line with a count, percentage, issue or PR id or commit SHA that the turn's material lacks is stripped, a Headline asked for once more, and a stripped second one is replaced by the response's first line. Numbers in the diff, the line counts or a check's result, number words and digits in a known path always pass.

A summary stored before the Headline is read with its first line as the Headline and the rest as `reported` lines; the writer is not run on it again.

**The evidence line**, under the Headline, states what the hooks and git recorded, with no model in it: `3 files · +2 merged · 2 checks passed`. A check counts by its last run, so a failure the agent fixed does not show; only a check that ended failed is named (`✗ cargo test failed`), and one that only "ran" is counted apart. A check is a suite in a worktree, so the same suite in two worktrees is two rows, and a row for a worktree that is not the session's own names it (`✗ cargo test in <worktree> failed`). A check still running, or one an agent sent to the background that has not reported, is not counted. One click opens it in place: each file with its line counts (+added −removed) and the agent whose call names it or whose worktree holds it, and each check with its status ("passed after 1 retry"). The files are every file that differs between the turn's trees, so a file changed through Bash counts like one changed by `Edit`, and a hand edit of yours during the turn shows as the turn's with no agent. A file is left out only when another session's or another turn's call touched the same worktree during the turn's trees and names it, and no call of the turn itself names it (a file the turn's own call names stays the turn's, as a merged file does): an `Edit` or `Write` of its absolute path, a Bash command whose text holds its absolute path, or its path from the root in a command that touched that root. A bare file name never matches, and no shell is parsed. With no other call in the worktree, every differing file is the turn's. There is no external count. A turn whose diff in one worktree holds more than 2,000 files, such as a leftover build directory, is skipped for that worktree with no rename detection run: with no other worktree to show it has no evidence line, and a `warn` line in the Studio log names its session, its turn and the file count (no path), and it is not diffed again until the turn ends and its end trees land. A deleted session's turns are not diffed until the session starts again. A turn with no recorded end, on an ended session or before a later turn, counts as over: it is never compared with the worktree as it is now, and the calls matched against it stop at the next turn's start, or at the session's last event.

**Merged files.** Work a merge brought in is not the turn's change. A file that a move of `HEAD` during the turn brought into the worktree (a merge, a pull, a rebase, a checkout or a reset, read from `HEAD`'s reflog; the agent's own commits are no move) is counted as "+N merged" on the evidence line, as a plain row in the opened list and in the full diff's head, and is neither listed as a file of the turn nor counted in its files. A merged file that a call of the turn names (an `Edit` or `Write` of its absolute path, or a Bash command whose text holds its path), such as a resolved conflict, stays the turn's. A turn's merge flag, which the card offer leaves out, is set by `git merge` or `gh pr merge`; `git merge-base` does not set it.

A failed check's row opens its exit code and the last 40 lines of its output. A running turn shows only a check that ended failed here; the checks that run or failed are listed in the feed.

**PR brief ready.** When a ready PR (open, not a draft) of the session's branch has a stored PR brief (`server/src/pr_briefs.rs`; [The Map view](map.md)), the newest turn shows one quiet row under the evidence line, "PR brief ready", with no colour and no count. Pressing it opens the Map view on that project and plays the brief from its first chapter (`ui/rail-brief.ts`). Only a live session has the row (not an `ended` one, nor one with no PR): a PR with no live studio session has none, and its brief is still in the Map view's tray and lights the project header's dot. The page asks the server (`briefs_waiting`) once for each PR number a session shows and again when the Map view is told a brief changed (`map-landed`), never on a timer. A PR whose touched parts would not fog at Daniel's tiers has no brief, so no row.

**The feed's checks** are the ones the core gives it, in [Checks](checks.md#the-feed): under a turn, the session's own agent's checks that are running (the time so far against the typical one) or failed, newest first. The rail draws them and keeps no rule of its own. A turn's files are the diff between the git trees the hook recorded at its start and end, less the files another session's or turn's call names (the rule of the evidence line); the ended turn's trees are kept under `refs/plotkeeper/turns/`, so `git gc` leaves its hunks. An agent that reports no trees (agy) has no evidence line and no hunks.

**Real code.** A file row opens its real hunk between the turn's trees, in place, at most about 40 lines; a capped hunk's "N more lines" opens the turn's whole diff, . The palette's "studio::OpenFullDiff" opens the whole diff of the newest turn that has files, in the centre column's place, one fold per file with its hunk read whole, and a button to go back. The page has no editor to open a file in.

**Earlier turns** fold to one line each: your prompt, the Headline and a mark for a check that ended failed. A click opens the turn's block under it and folds it again. Opening or closing the evidence line, a hunk or a folded turn changes only that node: the rail's others are kept, the scroll position does not move, and what is open stays open when the studio's state refreshes.

**A turn on the map** (spec #549, #560). Hovering or focusing a turn, the newest or an earlier one, lights on the Map view the parts it changed, its Workers' files included (see [The Map view](map.md)); leaving it clears them, and so does a redraw of the feed. Unfolding an earlier turn pans those parts clear of the panel, and the newest turn, already unfolded, has a quiet "Show on map" under its evidence line that does the same. A turn whose end (or start, if it never ended) is older than 7 days says "Files older than 7 days aren't shown on the map" in its block and lights nothing, but only for an agent that records turn files (its turn trees); a turn with no changed files lights nothing and has no "Show on map". While a PR brief plays, a turn's hover lights nothing, unfolding pans nothing and "Show on map" does nothing.

**Ask** sits on a file row, a failed check's row, a subagent's line and the Headline, and the palette's "studio::AskAbout" opens the newest turn's Headline box. Opening it shows what the step ledger and git recorded about the thing (for a file: your prompt, the steps that changed it and its hunk; for a check, its command, exit code and the end of its output; for a subagent, its prompt and steps; for the Headline, your prompt, its files and its checks), at once and with no model.

A suggested question or one you type goes to the writer's `claude -p` with those facts, and its answer shows under "Interpretation", apart from the recorded facts; the session never sees it. On a file or the Headline, "Why didn't it touch this?" takes a file or a name and shows what was recorded first (read, searched or mentioned in the turn), then the writer's possible reasons folded under "possible reasons (not recorded)"; with nothing recorded there are no reasons, and no call. There is no per-turn "ask about this".

**The top bar** holds the session's header (its state dot, state and name), which comes first, and a click on it toggles the rail, then the needs-you and unseen chips. Nothing else: usage, new session, the git line, restart and settings are not on the page.

When the window is too narrow for the rail beside the centre column (the width rule, unchanged), the rail is a layer over the centre column: resting the pointer on the session's header or on the spine at the window's left edge for 300 ms opens it, leaving it closes it after 400 ms, and a mouse button held down never opens it. A click pins it open; Esc and a click outside close it. A peek marks its turn seen only after 2 seconds open. The list's strip uses the same delays. The window's minimum width is 480 px.
