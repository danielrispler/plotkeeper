# The sessions

## The session list

On the right, the session list: every session (Claude Code, agy and Codex), one line each with the agent's logo, its name and its state, grouped by repository, a task's worktree under its main checkout's, and, inside a repository, by state with "needs you" first. The list holds what Home showed (#382): a row that needs you adds a second line, the open question's first line, else who asks (a plan, a permission, or a subagent: its name and "asks"); a running row its Intention, or its current step until the Intention is known; an unseen row (your turn or idle, not landed) its last turn's Headline, which updates when the turn's summary is stored. No other row has a second line. A second line is one line, muted, at 11.5 px, cut with an ellipsis. The selected row adds how long it has waited ("Bash cargo test · 3m"); a session whose last step is a wake-up reads "worker … returned" until its next step. The agent's logo is 12 px in one muted tone; colour in the list is the "Needs you" pill only. Hovering a session's row lights its agent chips on the map.

"Clear landed", quiet under "History · N", shows only while a landed session is in the list (#244, #382). It hides every landed row at once, ended ones included, from the list and the strip, and shows a toast, "Cleared N landed", with Undo for 8 seconds. Undo brings the rows back and nothing was deleted. When the 8 seconds pass, or the app quits inside them, each hidden session goes through Delete: its row drops, and its cards stay. The rows stay hidden until the sessions leave the list, so they never flash back before the delete lands, and a Clear in that gap does not commit them twice; a delete that fails brings its row back. A reload of the page forgets a pending clear. A session that stops being landed inside the window is left alone. The server hears each session until its delete is written, so a quit while the deletes are in flight still commits them. A second Clear inside the window adds its rows and restarts the 8 seconds.

Idle sessions collapse at the bottom; ended ones are in History, from the "History · N" link at the list's end or the palette's "Show history". The rest of the page is the map of a project (see [The Map view](map.md#the-map-view), #386): the session you click opens its story (see [The story rail](rail.md)) in the left panel over its project's map, and History, a turn's whole diff, a subagent's log and Settings open in the same panel. Only your click changes the session shown.

A session is running from a prompt until its turn ends; needs you while it waits on your permission, answer, plan approval or click on a blocked command (see [Blocked commands](cards.md#blocked-commands)); your turn when its turn ended without asking you, or it started and has had no prompt yet; idle after 2 hours at your turn with no events, until its next event; ended from its `SessionEnd` until it starts again under its id. A session also ends when its agent is gone (#330): the hook records `studio_pid` on each event, the pid of its parent, or its parent's parent when that is a shell (`sh`, `bash`, `zsh`, `dash`, `fish`), and agy's wrapper passes its own parent as `PLOTKEEPER_AGENT_PID`, which wins. About every 30 seconds the server checks each session that has not ended and has a pid (a signal 0 to it, which sends nothing), and appends a `SessionEnd` with reason `process_gone` for each whose process is gone, so a Ctrl+C or a closed herdr pane ends it within about 30 seconds. The lines the server writes itself (this `SessionEnd`, a closed terminal's, an interrupted turn's `StudioInterrupted`) are shaped like Claude Code's whatever the session's agent, so they never change its agent: an agy session ended or interrupted this way keeps agy's logo and transcript reader, and a restart replays it the same (#352). A session with no pid never ends this way: with no events for 2 hours, in a turn or waiting on Daniel as well as at your turn, it goes idle and its row says "Idle?"; its next event revives it. Which pid each harness gives is unverified until the herdr trial (#332): Claude Code's hook is its child, possibly through a shell; agy's wrapper `$PPID` is agy only if agy starts the wrapper itself. A harness whose recorded pid is not its agent's takes the wrong path: one that dies at once would end its sessions wrongly, one that outlives the agent would never end them, so the trial must confirm each. No hook payload carries a pid of its own.

A turn you interrupt with Esc fires no `Stop` and no `idle_prompt`: when its terminal reports OSC 7501 `idle` for 5 seconds while its hooks say it runs, the app appends a `StudioInterrupted` line for that turn, with its end trees, and the turn ends at your turn; outside a studio terminal the next prompt ends it. `done` never ends a turn, as a turn that finished says `done` before its `Stop` lands. A wake-up is not your prompt, so it leaves "needs you" in place. Only "needs you" is in colour. macOS shows a notification when a session newly needs you, a worker fails or it is your turn, while the window is not focused. While it is focused, a session that reaches your turn off screen (not selected and not in the split's second pane) raises a note over the terminal's top-right corner, under the needs-you toast: its name and its turn's intention ("3 finished" for several within 3 seconds), with the your-turn sound. The note is not in colour, fades after 6 seconds unless the pointer rests on it, and a click selects its session; a newer note replaces it. The top bar's unseen chip, beside the needs-you chip, counts the unseen sessions and selects the newest, as ⌘⇧U does.

A session whose context share passes `context_warning` (30% by default) raises the same note, "Context passed 30%: now 34%", with no sound and whether or not it is on screen, and its row's "context N%" turns from muted to full strength until the share falls back to the threshold or under. It warns once per crossing. A session already past it when the app starts raises no note; a changed threshold re-arms every session, so each one past the new threshold warns once.

A subagent's wait, failure or card shows on its session's line and names the subagent; a failed helper raises none. A subagent's name is its spawn's description, or the slash command of the forked skill that started it, such as `/code-review high origin/main...HEAD`, else the first 5 characters of its id; the list, its view's header, the notices and the card writer all use it. A session's folder is the last segment of its `cwd`, or the whole `cwd` when the folder ends in a slash. Notices within 3 seconds of the first show as one notification. The browser asks once for permission to show them, on your first press in the page. The tab's title counts the unseen sessions; it replaced the Dock badge (danielrispler/plotkeeper#331). A blocked command waits while its card has a button: Blocked, Blocked again and Allowed, not run count; an Allowed card, which waits for the agent's retry and not for you, does not.

A session with blocked commands waiting shows "N blocked commands waiting" ("1 blocked command waiting") as its row's second line in any state but ended, counting the cards that have a button (Blocked, Blocked again, Allowed, not run), until each is chosen (or declined). An Allowed card, Running, Finished or Running in background does not count. An open question still takes the line. A Bash command auto mode blocks while the app runs raises the notification "Auto mode blocked a command", naming the session and the subagent, under the same rules as the others: not while the window is focused, and combined with notices within 3 seconds. It replaces the "needs you" notice that block would raise. A block replayed at launch raises none.

### Projects

plotkeeper keeps its own list of projects, `projects.json` in the data folder (`server/src/projects.rs`, #381). A repository joins it the first time a session is seen in it, as its main checkout (a task's worktree joins its main checkout's project, the rule the list groups by). It stays after its sessions end or are deleted, and leaves only through Remove project, in the "⋯" menu of a project header with no live session (a project with live sessions would join again at their next event); a removed project comes back only when a session is active in it after the removal. The list's groups are read from it: every saved project has a header even with no live session, as its header only, after the projects with sessions, in the order they joined. A saved project whose folder was moved or deleted is gone: its header's name is struck through, and in the Map view it shows "This folder is gone", its path and Remove project where its map would be, never an error, and nothing reads its map.

## Home, retired

Home, the waiting room the page once opened on, is retired (#386, #388; [ADR 0001](../../adr/0001-retire-home.md)): the page opens on the map, and nothing draws Home. What it held lives in the list's second lines, Clear landed and the project header's dot.

## Sessions in the studio

The page starts no session: the picker, "+", the project registry ("Add project…"), Workspaces' rows, tasks, branches and "Pull", and the git line are gone from it (danielrispler/plotkeeper#326); sessions start in any terminal and record through the hook.

Cmd+Shift+H, once `studio::GoHome`, is now `studio::ShowMap` (#386), "back to the map", as Cmd+Shift+M is: it closes the left panel, which selects no session when it held a story. A `keymap.json` that still names `studio::GoHome` runs `studio::ShowMap`. A click on the list's empty space does the same. Cmd+J is unchanged.

"Rename" names the session: the server appends a `StudioRename` line to the event log, so the name survives a restart; the page types nothing into the session. An empty name gives the row its folder's again. "Open" and "Close", which resumed and ended a session's terminal, are gone with the terminals.

"Delete" asks, then drops the session's row: the server appends a `StudioDelete` line to the event log, so the row stays gone after a restart until a `SessionStart` of the session brings it back; its cards stay in the store, and no worktree or branch is removed. Clear landed, at the list's end, deletes the landed rows the same way once its Undo window has passed.

A late Allow of a blocked command reaches no terminal from the page: it shows the line to give the session to retry the command.

An agy session is recorded from any terminal, not only a studio one: this reverses #43's decision 9, which dropped an agy event that had no `PLOTKEEPER_TERMINAL_ID` because agy's plugin is global. Its place is `PLOTKEEPER_INTERNAL=1`: plotkeeper's own model runs (the outside review's agy, the card writer's `claude -p`) set it, and the hook drops every event from a process that carries it (#323). A session without a studio terminal has no terminal to link, so it shows as a plain session.
